## Story

```text
As a legal compliance officer,
I want a clearly labeled placeholder disclaimer on every screen where agents
see AI-generated content,
so that agents treat AI output as assistive, not binding legal advice, from
day one of the pilot.
```

## Notes

- **Notes:** The team chose a placeholder so that the pilot isn't blocked while
  Legal reviews the wording. The disclaimer is internal only and never appears
  in customer messages.
- **Assumptions:** [A-9], [A-11].

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-07.1** A disclaimer banner appears on every screen that shows
  AI-generated content **[TBD: list of screens]**.
- **AC-07.2** The banner shows placeholder wording that is clearly labeled
  **"DRAFT – pending Legal approval"**.
- **AC-07.3** Agents can't dismiss or hide the banner.
- **AC-07.4** The banner text is loaded from a single managed setting, so it
  can be changed without a code release.
- **AC-07.5** The disclaimer is **not** included in any text copied by
  quick-copy or otherwise prepared for customers (internal only, X-1).

## Assumptions needing validation

- **A-9** Running the pilot with a clearly labeled DRAFT disclaimer is acceptable to Legal and Compliance. *(The PM team accepted this; Legal hasn't confirmed it yet.)* _(validate with: Legal)_
- **A-11** An internal-only disclaimer is enough for this pilot. *(This follows from the scope decision, but Legal should confirm it.)* _(validate with: Legal)_
