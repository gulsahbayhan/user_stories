## Story

```text
As a support team lead,
I want flagged answers to be blocked from quick-copy until an authorized
reviewer approves them,
so that unreviewed answers cannot be sent by accident.
```

## Notes

- **Notes:** Stays at `Draft` until the review workflow is defined.
- **Open questions:** [OQ-5] reviewer role, turnaround target, rejection path.
- **Assumptions:** [A-7], [A-8].

## Acceptance criteria

> Stays at `Draft` until OQ-5 (reviewer role, turnaround, rejection path) is
> answered.

- **AC-06.1** On a flagged answer, the quick-copy button is disabled and a
  tooltip explains why.
- **AC-06.2** A user with the reviewer role **[TBD]** can approve a flagged
  answer. After approval, quick-copy is enabled for that answer.
- **AC-06.3** A user without the reviewer role can't approve flagged answers.
- **AC-06.4** Each approval is logged with the reviewer ID, timestamp, and
  answer ID.
- **AC-06.5** The agent can see the review status of a flagged answer
  (`Pending review` / `Approved`).
- **AC-06.6** What happens when a reviewer **rejects** an answer is **[TBD,
  OQ-5]**. It must be defined before this story is Ready.

## Assumptions needing validation

- **A-7** Reviewers have enough capacity to keep review turnaround acceptable. _(validate with: Support team lead)_
- **A-8** Blocking quick-copy is a meaningful control even though agents can still retype the text. _(validate with: Support team lead / Compliance)_
