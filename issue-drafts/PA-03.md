## Story

```text
As a policy content owner,
I want the assistant to use only documents currently marked as approved,
so that agents never see answers based on draft or retired policy.
```

## Notes

- **Notes:** Proposed by the agent and accepted by the PM. It turns the
  scenario constraint "approved policy documents only" into a testable story.
- **Assumptions:** [A-2]. The role "policy content owner" exists and someone
  is named in it, which is also part of [A-2].

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-03.1** Only documents marked "approved" in the system of record
  **[TBD system, A-2]** can be retrieved by the assistant.
- **AC-03.2** When a document is marked retired or draft, it's excluded from
  retrieval within **[TBD]** hours.
- **AC-03.3** A test question that matches **only** a retired or draft
  document triggers the PA-01 "No approved source found" fallback.
- **AC-03.4** Before the pilot starts, the policy content owner has signed off
  the list of documents in the pilot set.

## Assumptions needing validation

- **A-2** There is a single system of record for document approval status and version, with a named policy content owner. _(validate with: Policy / knowledge management)_
