## Story

```text
As a support agent,
I want to report an AI answer as wrong, outdated, or unhelpful in one step,
so that problems reach the team lead and policy owner quickly.
```

## Notes

- **Notes:** Proposed by the agent and accepted by the PM. It gives the pilot a direct signal
  about answer risk, alongside PA-09.
- **Assumptions:** [A-2] (the policy owner who receives reports exists).

## Acceptance criteria

- **AC-10.1** Each AI answer has a "Report issue" control.
- **AC-10.2** The agent picks a reason (`Incorrect`, `Outdated source`, `Wrong
  source`, `Unhelpful`, `Other`) and can add an optional comment. Submitting
  takes no more than two clicks, not counting the comment.
- **AC-10.3** Submitted reports appear in a list that the support team lead
  and policy content owner can see.
- **AC-10.4** Each report is logged as an event (PA-09) and linked to the
  answer ID and its cited sources.

## Assumptions needing validation

- **A-2** There is a single system of record for document approval status and version, with a named policy content owner. _(validate with: Policy / knowledge management)_
